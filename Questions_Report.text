1. Which insights did you gain from your EDA? Were any columns
highly correlated?

My EDA surfaced a few clear patterns. The dataset is genre-imbalanced — pop, country, and blues dominate while hip hop is underrepresented. Looking at correlations, the strongest pair was len ↔ obscene at ~0.44: longer songs tend to score higher on obscene/lifestyle content, a pattern likely driven by hip hop tracks being both verbose and explicit. I also saw meaningful negative correlations — obscene trends opposite to sadness and world/life (explicit songs and emotional songs tell different stories), and len ↔ age is negative, suggesting newer songs have longer lyrics.
Crucially, no feature pair exceeded |r| > 0.5, so there were no strongly correlated columns and I did not need to drop features on statistical grounds alone. The columns I did drop — family/spiritual and feelings — were removed during preprocessing for conceptual redundancy, since they measure themes already captured by family/gospel, sadness, and romantic. No need to tell the algorithm the same thing twice.


2. How did you determine which columns to drop or keep? If your EDA
informed this process, explain which insights you used to determine
which columns were not needed.

I dropped columns for three reasons. I dropped lyrics because it's raw text and KMeans can't use it, and topic because it's a categorical label that would leak cluster structure into an unsupervised task. I dropped family/spiritual and feelings because they carry the same information already captured by family/gospel, sadness, and romantic — no need to tell the algorithm the same thing twice. 

3. What was the optimal number of clusters in your cluster model?
Explain how you determined this value.

I used the elbow method testing k=2 to k=15. The plot showed a general bend somewhere between k=7 and k=10, but it wasn't clear enough to pick a definitive value. So I applied the Silhouette score to get a sharper answer — it measures how well each song fits its own cluster versus neighboring clusters. Based on the Silhouette score peaking at k=10, I decided that was the optimal number of clusters.
